# Content Roadmap — Upgrade package

## Included
- START / N5 / N4 / N3 / N2 / N1 scaffold
- 240 vocabulary entries
- 120 grammar structures with Vietnamese explanation and examples
- 10 themed units per level in the learning path
- Integrated unit view: vocabulary selection, grammar point, short reading, browser audio and completion
- word-based Kanji collection (using the reading/meaning of a word)
- Kana, reading samples, listening samples, quizzes, marked-word review, progress and optional Supabase

## Counts in the initial corpus
- START: 40 vocabulary, 20 grammar
- N5: 40 vocabulary, 20 grammar
- N4: 40 vocabulary, 20 grammar
- N3: 40 vocabulary, 20 grammar
- N2: 40 vocabulary, 20 grammar
- N1: 40 vocabulary, 20 grammar

## Not yet a complete textbook corpus
These counts are a starting content pack, not comprehensive level inventories. The package does not yet contain the thousands of words, hundreds/thousands of Kanji, grammar points, long readings, natural audio recordings and fully timed official-format mock exams needed for a genuinely complete N5–N1 school-style curriculum. Content is original, not copied from copyrighted textbooks.

## Planned expansion
1. Expand vocab and Kanji to validated level-specific target inventories.
2. Add word-level details (parts of speech, collocations, synonyms/antonyms, pitch accent where validated).
3. Add grammar points with formation, nuance, contrasts, restrictions and multiple examples.
4. Add lesson-specific exercises with answer explanations.
5. Add reading passages and listening audio with scripts and questions.
6. Add full mock-exam section timers, scoring and performance analytics.
7. Implement spaced repetition scheduling and robust account/cloud backup.
